Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Necromancer's Rise does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, or other directly-identifying personal information. Your game progress is saved locally on your device and backed up to the cloud under a randomly generated anonymous identifier — not your real identity — so it can be restored if you reinstall or switch devices.

The app uses the following services, which may process data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — provides anonymous cloud save/sync and the optional PvP ladder and in-game chat. Save data and chat messages are stored against that anonymous identifier, not your name or email.
  • In-game chat — messages you send are visible to other players and pass through a profanity filter; you can block any player at any time. Please don't share personal information in chat.
  • Google AdMob (advertising) — may use device identifiers to serve ads. For users in the EEA/UK, ad personalization is governed by the consent choice shown in-app. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing (Android in-app purchases) — processes purchases through your Google account. We never see or store your payment details.
  • Apple App Store (iOS in-app purchases) — processes purchases through your Apple Account. We never see or store your payment details.

The game does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of consent. You can reset your advertising preferences at any time from your device settings.
